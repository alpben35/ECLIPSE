Questions about Eclipse

Who is it for?

Students studying mathematics, science, English and other subjects, from primary school through university. Choose questions appropriate to your course.

Can I use Eclipse for free?

The free plan includes 15 AI prompts per day. View the plans page for current paid allowances and prices.

Is every AI answer correct?

No. AI can make mistakes. Verify important answers with your teacher, textbook or another trusted source. Eclipse supports learning and does not replace a qualified teacher.

What should I share?

Share the question you need help with. Keep your full name, school, address, contact details and other people's information out of messages and uploads.
